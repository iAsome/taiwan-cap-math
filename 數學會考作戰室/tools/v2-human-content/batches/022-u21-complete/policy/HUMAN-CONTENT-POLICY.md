# Human Content Policy

- Every lecture, multiple-choice item, constructed-response item, review, and drawing specification is independently authored for U21.
- `contentAuthority` must be `CHATGPT_HUMAN_AUTHORED_R1`.
- Legacy production questions, lectures, reviews, generators, and compiled banks are untrusted and were not used.
- Cursor is implementation-only and may not generate, rewrite, or repair mathematical content.
- Production activation is prohibited until the central coordinator completes all-unit acceptance and the global release gate.
- The delivered ZIP is immutable source and must not contain `staging/`; staging is emitted only after verification in an approved integration workspace.

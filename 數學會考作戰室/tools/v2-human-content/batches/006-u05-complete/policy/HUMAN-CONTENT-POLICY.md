# U05 Human Content Policy

- Authority marker: `CHATGPT_HUMAN_AUTHORED_R1`
- Every lecture, multiple-choice item and constructed-response item was authored for this package.
- Old production questions, lectures, generated reviews and compiled banks are untrusted and were not used.
- Code in this package may serialize reviewed records, calculate hashes, validate invariants and emit staging modules. It must not generate or repair mathematical content.
- Production activation is forbidden.
- Legacy deletion is forbidden in this batch.
- No skill may be added, removed, split, merged or reordered.
- U05 is limited to Taiwan junior-high CAP coordinate geometry. Do not introduce trigonometry, vectors, matrices, conic sections or high-school analytic geometry.
- The delivery ZIP intentionally contains no `staging/` directory.

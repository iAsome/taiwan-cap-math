# HUMAN CONTENT POLICY — U16 BATCH 017

1. Every lecture, multiple-choice item and constructed-response item is individually authored and individually reviewed.
2. Mathematical templates, number swapping, cosmetic story changes and parameterized question factories are prohibited.
3. Code in this package may serialize reviewed records, calculate hashes, validate invariants, emit staging modules and scan duplicates. It may not author or repair content.
4. Every teaching record carries `contentAuthority: "CHATGPT_HUMAN_AUTHORED_R1"`.
5. Legacy production questions, lectures, generated reviews, compiled banks and generators are untrusted and were not used.
6. A figure is included only where geometry or construction structure materially improves understanding. Every SVG has `<title>`, `<desc>`, `role="img"`, a detailed drawing specification and manual review.
7. Production activation remains forbidden until all 23 units pass the global final release gate.
8. The delivery ZIP must not contain `staging/`.
9. Cross-unit duplicate resolution belongs to the central coordinator because other unit packages were authored concurrently.

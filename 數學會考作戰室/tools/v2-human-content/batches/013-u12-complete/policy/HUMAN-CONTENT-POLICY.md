# Human Content Policy — Batch 013 U12

1. Every lecture, MC item, constructed-response item, and review is authoritative only when marked `CHATGPT_HUMAN_AUTHORED_R1`.
2. Mathematical prose and records must be individually authored; number-swapped templates, parameterized factories, legacy transformations, and circular review evidence are prohibited.
3. Code in this package may serialize reviewed records, calculate canonical hashes, validate invariants, emit non-production staging modules, and scan duplicates. It may not invent or repair mathematical content.
4. The syllabus lock controls IDs, order, prerequisite links, and CAP boundaries. Its generic objectives and misconceptions are not teaching content.
5. Traditional Chinese used in Taiwan is required for teaching content. Internal titles must not contain `、`.
6. A correct MC item requires exactly one true choice, four specific option analyses, an independently recomputed answer, and question-specific semantic review.
7. A constructed response requires a 0–3 question-specific rubric, partial-credit policy, arithmetic-error follow-through policy, unit/notation policy, and answer-only policy.
8. Figures are included only where they materially support the mathematics and require exact drawing specifications, alt text, SVG title/description, mobile inspection, and answer-leakage review.
9. Production activation, legacy deletion, and runtime replacement remain forbidden until the global release gate passes.

# Math V2 Human Content Policy R1

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Every lecture and every question is authored as an individual content record.
2. Loops may serialize, validate, hash, sort, or package records. They may not generate question prose, options, explanations, examples, difficulty, or review decisions.
3. Cursor may copy files, run validators, emit modules, and report diffs. Cursor may not invent, rewrite, shorten, expand, or repair mathematical content.
4. A review record is valid only when it contains an independently derived result and a truth evaluation for every option.
5. `answerIndex`, stored answers, hashes, and prior review statuses may not be used as the source of an independent answer.
6. A literacy context must impose a real operational condition. Decorative names, locations, or stories do not qualify.
7. A figure question requires a question-specific drawing specification. No generic figure template may substitute for missing geometry.
8. No content is student-facing until the complete batch passes schema, hash, semantic completeness, duplicate, and manual review gates.

## Cursor boundary

Allowed: exact copying, UTF-8 preservation, schema validation, deterministic conversion, build, tests, diff report.
Forbidden: content generation, paraphrasing, auto-filling, difficulty assignment, answer inference, auto-pass reviews, drawing interpretation beyond an explicit drawing specification.

# HUMAN CONTENT POLICY — Batch 019 U18

## Authority

All lectures, multiple-choice questions, constructed-response questions, solutions, option analyses, rubrics, semantic reviews and drawing specifications in this package are individually authored under `CHATGPT_HUMAN_AUTHORED_R1`.

## Prohibitions

Old production content is untrusted. It must not be copied, paraphrased, repaired, mined, remixed or used as inspiration. Parameterized question factories, number-only substitutions, generic review evidence, circular answer checking and automated mathematical content generation are prohibited.

Code in this package may only validate reviewed records, calculate hashes, check invariants and serialize reviewed source into batch-local staging modules.

## Review requirements

Every MC item has four unique choices, one correct answer, an independent solution, all-choice analysis, ambiguity and boundary audit, difficulty reason and question-specific semantic review. Every constructed response has a question-specific 0–3 rubric, partial-credit rules, follow-through policy and independent review. Every lecture includes prerequisite bridging, first-principles development, formulas with conditions, invalid uses, examples, concrete mistakes, self-checks, connections and a skill-specific review.

## Isolation

`legacyContentImported=false`, `legacySemanticEvidenceAccepted=false`, `oldProductionRuntimeModified=false`, `deleteLegacyNow=false`, `finalRetirementRequired=true`, and `productionActivationAllowed=false`.

The central coordinator alone controls final integration, activation and old-database retirement.

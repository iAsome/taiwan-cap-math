# Question and assessment standard

## Atomic-skill bank
At least 12 unique, static, individually reviewed questions per skill:
- 3 foundation/recognition;
- 4 standard application;
- 3 multi-step, data, or inference;
- 2 transfer/high-discrimination.

Adjust the mix when a subject construct requires different forms, but cover every level.

## Item record
Every item contains stable ID, exact final stem/stimulus/options, answer, full rationale, per-distractor rationale, skill/curriculum/source mapping, misconception target, difficulty, cognitive process, representation type, originality record, review hashes, and accessibility metadata.

## Prohibitions
- no ambiguous key;
- no trick based only on wording defect;
- no implausible throwaway distractors;
- no “all/none of the above” unless construct-justified;
- no answer-position patterns;
- no copied textbook or commercial question;
- no substitution templates counted as unique items;
- no unsupported current-event or law claim;
- no figure whose answer depends on inaccessible color alone;
- no rationale that merely repeats the answer.

## Validation
Independently solve every item twice. Numerical/scientific items require unit/dimension and edge-case checks. Language items require naturalness, grammar, semantic, context, and alternative-answer checks. Social items require date/place/source and viewpoint checks.

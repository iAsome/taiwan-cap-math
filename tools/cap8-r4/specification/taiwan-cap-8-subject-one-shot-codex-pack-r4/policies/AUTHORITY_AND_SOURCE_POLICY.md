# Official-curriculum authority and source policy

## Binding scope
The current official fourth-learning-stage curriculum learning performance, learning content, appendices, reference tables, issue integration, and inquiry/practice requirements define the mandatory scope.

Current CAP subject specifications, Q&A, level/scoring descriptions, corrections, and accommodations govern assessment-facing implementation. Official 106–115 papers calibrate item forms and demand; they do not replace curriculum coverage.

## Publisher references
Publisher lectures and lawful public resources are secondary references only. Record publisher, edition/date, title, URL/path, access level, hash, license status, curriculum codes observed, teaching-sequence observations, terminology, examples/representations, misconceptions, and adoption/rejection decision.

Publisher material may reveal a teaching omission but cannot override an official node. A point appearing only in a publisher source is enrichment unless independently supported by official authority or official-paper evidence.

## Factual claims
Each source record includes agency/publisher, title, version/date, retrieval date, URL/path, hash, license, subject/scope, and reviewer decision. Current laws, statistics, boundaries, institutional facts, and scientific conventions require current primary-source verification.

Search snippets, anonymous sites, cram-school summaries, and generated text are discovery aids only. Conflicting claims require a resolution file; silent selection is prohibited.

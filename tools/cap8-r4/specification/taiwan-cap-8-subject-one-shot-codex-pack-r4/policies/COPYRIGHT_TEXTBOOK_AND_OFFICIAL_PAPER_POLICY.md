# Copyright, publisher-reference, and official-paper policy

Publisher materials are teaching references, not content to reproduce. Record lawful metadata and observations, but use full text/assets only with documented permission or a clear lawful basis.

For unlicensed modern material:
- do not copy paragraphs, questions, answer explanations, figures, maps, photos, worksheets, or audio;
- create independent explanations, original examples, original passages, original parallel questions, and original accessible assets;
- preserve only necessary source metadata and high-level coverage observations.

Modern Chinese literary texts require permission, lawful short quotation, or replacement. Public-domain classical texts require edition collation, punctuation, variant-character, annotation, translation, and source review.

Official CAP papers may be stored/linked and audited under applicable official terms. Keep official items separate from original production banks and identify them clearly as official historical materials.

Every final artifact must have provenance, originality, license/copyright status, and final-audit evidence. Replace any doubtful artifact rather than blocking all other work.

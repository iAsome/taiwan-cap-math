# Official 106–115 exhaustive audit policy

For each year 106 through 115 inclusive, obtain all available official paper, answer, correction, explanation, listening, writing, and scoring-example materials. Record URL, retrieval time, hash, file type, parser/render method, and any discrepancies.

Create one row per item and sub-item. Inspect original rendered pages/audio, not extracted text alone. Record:
- paper/section/item locator;
- shared stimulus and asset links;
- complete option/answer structure;
- official answer and correction status;
- primary/secondary subject and integrated tags;
- curriculum codes and atomic skills;
- representation/source type;
- cognitive operations and misconception/distractor observations;
- final audit status.

English listening requires audio/script/playback verification. Social and Natural require cross-domain classification. Writing requires prompt and scoring-support analysis.

Existing site labels are hypotheses only. Re-read every source item. Use findings to calibrate original production and transparent trend analysis; do not copy official items into original practice banks.

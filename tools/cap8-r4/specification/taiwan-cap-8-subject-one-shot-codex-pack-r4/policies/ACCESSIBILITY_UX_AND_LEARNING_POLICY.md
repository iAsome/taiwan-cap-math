# Accessibility, UX, and learning policy

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Require semantic headings, landmarks, labels, visible focus, keyboard-only completion, sufficient contrast, no color-only meaning, reduced motion, scalable text, touch target adequacy, accessible dialogs/timers, captions/transcripts, alt text/long descriptions, and printable alternatives.

Learning flow must support diagnostic entry, prerequisites, explain-example-practice-retrieve-transfer, mastery thresholds, spaced review, mistake classification, targeted remediation, and resumable progress. Never shame learners or fabricate predicted scores.

Timers must be pausable in practice mode and strict only in exam mode. Official-style modes clearly distinguish official rules from site-defined subsubject practice.

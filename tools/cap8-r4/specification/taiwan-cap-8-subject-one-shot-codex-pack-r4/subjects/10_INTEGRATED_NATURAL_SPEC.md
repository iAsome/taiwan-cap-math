# Integrated Natural Science exam specification

Activate only after Biology, Physics/Chemistry, and Earth Science pass.

- Official-style four-option exam, configurable within current official 45–55 question range, default based on the latest verified paper, 70 minutes.
- Blueprint is calibrated from complete 106–115 ledgers.
- Assess scientific cognition, inquiry ability, and attitudes/nature of science where paper-and-pencil assessment permits.
- Include experiments, controlled variables, apparatus, diagrams, data tables, graphs, model evaluation, units, uncertainty, environmental/energy/ocean/disaster contexts, and genuine cross-domain systems.
- Preserve subdiscipline and inquiry-skill remediation.

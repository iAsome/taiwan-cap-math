# Earth Science complete-production specification

## Construct
Earth Science is part of the combined Natural Science exam. Use system interactions, maps, cross-sections, weather charts, satellite data, geological evidence, astronomical scale, and hazard reasoning. Distinguish observation, model, inference, forecast, and uncertainty.

## Minimum production floor
- Atomic skills and full lectures: 220
- Static skill questions: 2640 (minimum 12 per skill)
- Additional stimulus-based questions: 660
- Counts are floors, not ceilings.

2. Minerals and identification properties
3. Igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks
4. Rock cycle and Earth materials
5. Earth interior and seismic evidence
6. Plate tectonics and boundary processes
7. Earthquakes, waves and hazard interpretation
8. Volcanoes and volcanic hazards
9. Geologic time, fossils and relative dating
10. Weathering, erosion, transport and deposition
11. Rivers, groundwater and water resources
12. Oceans, currents, waves and coasts
13. Atmosphere structure and composition
14. Weather elements and instruments
15. Air pressure, wind and circulation
16. Air masses, fronts and weather maps
17. Typhoons, monsoons and severe weather
18. Climate systems and climate graphs
19. Taiwan geology and natural hazards
20. Earth-Moon-Sun motions
21. Day/night, seasons and calendars
22. Moon phases, eclipses and tides
23. Solar system objects and scale
24. Stars, brightness, color and life cycles
25. Galaxies, universe and observation
26. Astronomical instruments and sky maps
27. Space exploration and satellites
28. Climate change evidence and mechanisms
29. Disaster prevention and sustainability
30. Integrated maps, satellite data, graphs and system reasoning

## Required workflow
1. Parse all applicable official curriculum performance/content codes and appendices.
2. Audit current approved textbook editions and lawful samples/errata.
3. Re-read and classify every applicable 106–115 official item.
4. Split topic families into atomic teachable skills; do not combine unrelated concepts.
5. Author source-of-truth lectures and questions.
6. Build required original figures/maps/audio/data sets.
7. Complete authoring QA, freeze the artifact, then complete the single final exhaustive semantic audit at the exact final hash.
8. Build and test subject runtime and migrate existing progress.
9. Pass subject gate before activating the next subject.

# Biology complete-production specification

## Construct
Biology is part of the combined Natural Science exam. Integrate conceptual knowledge with experiment design, variables, diagrams, data tables, graphs, systems, evidence, and environmental/health contexts. Avoid anthropomorphic or teleological shortcuts that create misconceptions.

## Minimum production floor
- Atomic skills and full lectures: 220
- Static skill questions: 2640 (minimum 12 per skill)
- Additional stimulus-based questions: 660
- Counts are floors, not ceilings.

9. Circulation and immunity basics
10. Excretion and internal balance

12. Endocrine regulation

23. Plant structure and transport

25. Population, community and ecosystem
26. Food chains, webs and energy flow
27. Matter cycles and ecological balance
28. Biodiversity conservation
29. Human health and environmental impact
30. Integrated experiment, graph, diagram and inference

## Required workflow
1. Parse all applicable official curriculum performance/content codes and appendices.
2. Audit current approved textbook editions and lawful samples/errata.
3. Re-read and classify every applicable 106–115 official item.
4. Split topic families into atomic teachable skills; do not combine unrelated concepts.
5. Author source-of-truth lectures and questions.
6. Build required original figures/maps/audio/data sets.
7. Complete authoring QA, freeze the artifact, then complete the single final exhaustive semantic audit at the exact final hash.
8. Build and test subject runtime and migrate existing progress.
9. Pass subject gate before activating the next subject.

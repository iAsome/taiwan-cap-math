# Official source acquisition requirements

Revalidate all URLs and versions at execution time. Download/hash official curriculum documents and appendices, CAP subject specifications/Q&A/descriptors/corrections, and all available 106–115 materials. Use PDF page rendering/screenshot inspection for figures, maps, tables, and layouts; use audio inspection for listening.

Maintain source provenance and parser/render versions. Preserve raw files where lawful and necessary for reproducibility. If an official page moves, locate the current responsible-agency source and record redirects/replacement evidence.

Publisher references are acquired separately under the publisher-reference policy and never substitute for missing official scope authority.
